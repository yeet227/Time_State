# Time-State 3D Development Log
Legend:
WIP means Work In Progress
MNH means Might Not Happen, Because the state of the project is consistently changing
Completed obviously means that the feature has been made and is complete, but still may contain bugs or issues during gameplay

Completed Features (Avenue 4: Dieselpunk Chrono-Wasteland RPG):
- Drivable Wasteland Cruiser Vehicle (Completed)
  * Real-time arcade vehicle controller with suspension and terrain slope alignment
  * Full driving controls: W/S throttle & reverse, A/D steering, Space drift/handbrake, Shift Nitro Chrono-Boost
  * Enter/Exit vehicle mechanics ([E] key) with seamless camera transfer
  * Vehicle ramming physics dealing high-impact damage to hostile wasteland mobs
  * Vehicle headlights for night driving and Nitro exhaust particle effects
- Multi-Weapon Arsenal & Melee Combat (Completed)
  * Slot 1: Energy Blaster (ranged projectile sidearm, consumes Energy Ammo)
  * Slot 2: Wasteland Sword (fast steel slashing blade, stamina-based melee sweeps)
  * Slot 3: Scrapper Battleaxe (heavy overhead cleave with massive knockback and damage)
  * Hotkey weapon switching (1, 2, 3) and mouse wheel cycling
  * Weapon HUD box displaying active weapon stats and damage
- Chrono Dilation / Bullet Time (Completed)
  * [Q] toggle slows world time down to 0.32x while maintaining player real-time agility
  * Temporal screen border vignette pulse and slow-mo HUD indicator
  * Stamina-drain balance mechanism
- Wasteland Faction Landmarks & Discovery System (Completed)
  * Scrapper Outpost & Garage (Scrappers Coalition)
  * The Imperial Citadel (Ancient Dynasty)
  * Sanctuary of the Church (The Faithful Order)
  * Tank Graveyard & Siren Outpost (Iron Legion Remnants)
  * Dynamic proximity discovery banner and faction territory announcements
- Enemy Archetypes & 3D Visual Diversity (Completed)
  * Wasteland Raiders (balanced humanoid threats)
  * Bouncing Gremlins (high-speed leaping melee swarmers using custom 3D model)
  * Floating Chrono Drones (aerial hover snipers using custom 3D model)
  * Heavy Automatons (armored tanky minibosses guarding vaults)
- UI Suite & Full In-Game HUD (Completed)
  * Health bar, Stamina bar, Energy Ammo counter, Speed/Multiplier readouts
  * Interactive Chest Storage transfer modal
  * Save & Load game persistence (F5/F9)
  * Full Settings (FOV, Sensitivity, VSync, Fullscreen, Volume)
  * Debug Cheat Menu (F1) and flight mode (V)
- Dynamic Rotating Day/Night Cycle (Completed)
- GDScript Code Flaws & Systems Bug Audit (Completed)
  * Launch Pads & Jump Pads: Fixed upward velocity cancellation caused by ground snap logic in Player.gd; snap_vector is now cleared on upward impulse so players launch cleanly into the air.
  * Pickups & Drops: Fixed dynamic item drops (from defeated enemies) snapping down to Y=0 due to early _base_y initialization before world translation was set.
  * Enemy Spawner Logic: Set translation before add_child in World_Manager.gd and Test_Scene_Script.gd to prevent enemies from wandering back towards (0,0,0) instead of their outposts.
  * Chrono Dilation / Bullet Time Lifecycle: Guaranteed Engine.time_scale and bullet time flags are cleanly reset on player death/respawn, scene load, and returning to the Main Menu.
  * Vehicle Driver Decoupling: Prevented invisible driver player from continuing to process on-foot movement, gravity falling, stamina consumption, and shooting while inside the Wasteland Cruiser; driver coordinates now follow vehicle.
  * Vehicle Wheel Animation: Eliminated Euler gimbal flip and pitch/yaw fighting by using continuous _wheel_spin tracking.
  * Vehicle Terrain Traversal: Fixed uphill speed stalling by incorporating forward incline component into velocity on ground contact and switched to rotate_object_local for slope turning.
  * 3rd Person Camera & Character Turning: Set SpringArm to top-level with independent orbit, and added smooth character body rotation to face movement and aim directions.
  * Inventory Weapons: Added use_item_at handlers for sword_weapon and axe_prototype in Global.gd so weapons found in chests can be equipped directly from backpack.
  * Projectile Collision & Knockback: Passed source position on player projectiles so enemies take directional knockback, and broadened collision checks to all PhysicsBody instances (preventing shots passing through vehicles).
  * Billboard Health Bars: Set BILLBOARD_FIXED_Y on enemy floating health bars so they remain visible and legible from any camera angle.
  * Landmark Vehicle Triggers: Allowed driven vehicles to trigger landmark discovery zones.
  * Removed duplicate/malformed file "Scripts/Object_Rotate .gd" with trailing space.
